Our Story

Passion, family & a genuine wood-fired oven

Ristorante Pizzeria Paradiso (formerly Da Toni) celebrates a culture of passion, enthusiasm and attention to detail. We strive to use organic ingredients and source local produce where possible, holding ourselves accountable — as a family and as a business — to sustainable practices, while constantly delivering a superior dining experience.

2011An outlet for our creativity,
true to the authentic Italian experience

Paradiso is proud to employ multicultural chefs. Combined with our family and local staff, we've found the perfect balance of authenticity, connection with Fiordland, and our personal touch.

Genuine Atmosphere

The centrepiece of our restaurant is our genuine Italian wood-fired pizza oven. We've made it part of the atmosphere — our guests love watching the chefs at work.

Local & Sustainable

Organic ingredients where we can, local produce where possible — from Stewart Island salmon to fresh Fiordland venison.
